# Final Project Review Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction
### 1.1 Purpose
This document outlines the requirements for conducting a comprehensive review of the final project. The review aims to evaluate the project's success against its objectives, identify lessons learned, and provide recommendations for future improvements.

### 1.2 Scope
The review will cover:
- Project objectives and deliverables
- Key milestones and timelines
- Stakeholder engagement
- Budget and resource allocation
- Risk management outcomes
- Final product quality and performance

### 1.3 Definitions
- **Final Project**: The culmination of all planned activities, deliverables, and outcomes defined in the project charter.
- **Review**: A structured evaluation process to assess project success, compliance with requirements, and areas for improvement.

## 2. Objectives
1. Validate that the project met its stated objectives and KPIs.
2. Identify strengths and weaknesses in project execution.
3. Document lessons learned for future projects.
4. Provide actionable recommendations for process improvement.
5. Ensure alignment with organizational goals and stakeholder expectations.

## 3. Stakeholders
| Stakeholder | Role | Requirements |
|-------------|------|--------------|
| Project Team | Executed the project | Provide documentation, data, and insights |
| Project Sponsor | Funded the project | Review outcomes against business goals |
| End-Users | Beneficiaries of deliverables | Validate usability and satisfaction |
| Management | Oversight and decision-making | Receive analysis and recommendations |
| External Partners | Provided resources/services | Confirm compliance with contractual obligations |

## 4. Functional Requirements
### 4.1 Project Evaluation
- Assess completion of all deliverables against the project charter.
- Evaluate adherence to timelines and budget constraints.
- Analyze quality of outputs using predefined metrics.

### 4.2 Stakeholder Feedback
- Conduct interviews or surveys with stakeholders to gather qualitative feedback.
- Identify gaps between expected and actual outcomes.

### 4.3 Risk and Issue Review
- Review resolved risks and unresolved issues.
- Assess the effectiveness of risk management strategies.

### 4.4 Documentation Review
- Verify completeness and accuracy of project documentation (e.g., plans, reports, logs).
- Ensure traceability of requirements to deliverables.

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements
### 5.1 Quality Standards
- All findings must be accurate, objective, and supported by data.
- Recommendations must be actionable and prioritized.

### 5.2 Timelines
- The review must be completed within [X] weeks post-project delivery.
- Key milestones: 
  - Data collection: [Date]
  - Analysis: [Date]
  - Final report submission: [Date]

### 5.3 Resource Allocation
- Require collaboration from project managers, analysts, and stakeholders.
- Allocate [X] hours for data collection and analysis.

## 6. Constraints
- **Time**: Review must be completed by [specific date].
- **Budget**: Limited to [X] hours of analyst time.
- **Resources**: Access to project documentation and stakeholder contacts is required.

## 7. Assumptions
- All project documentation is available and up-to-date.
- Stakeholders will provide timely feedback.
- No major unresolved issues remain from the project lifecycle.

## 8. Deliverables
1. **Final Review Report**: Summarizing findings, analysis, and recommendations.
2. **Lessons Learned Document**: Structured insights for future projects.
3. **Stakeholder Feedback Summary**: Aggregated qualitative input.
4. **Recommendation Action Plan**: Prioritized next steps for improvement.
