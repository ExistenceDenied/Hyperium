# Hyperium Core Domain

> Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the core business concepts of Hyperium.

It establishes the ubiquitous language used throughout the platform.

Every software component should reflect these concepts.

---

# Core Principle

Hyperium is a Digital Consultancy Operating System.

It supports consultants by combining professional methodologies, reusable knowledge and AI capabilities.

AI is an implementation detail.

Consultancy is the domain.

---

# Business Domain

## Discipline

A professional area of expertise.

Examples:

- Business Analysis
- Enterprise Architecture
- Project Management
- Compliance
- Data & AI
- Software Engineering
- Testing

A Discipline defines:

- vocabulary
- techniques
- deliverable types
- roles
- knowledge
- best practices

---

## Methodology

A structured way of working within a Discipline.

Examples:

- BABOK
- TOGAF
- Scrum
- Hyperium BA
- Hyperium EA

A Methodology defines:

- principles
- lifecycle
- techniques
- quality gates
- deliverable types

---

## Lifecycle

The sequence of stages used by a methodology.

Examples:

- Discover
- Analyse
- Design
- Validate
- Implement
- Handover

---

## Stage

A logical phase within a lifecycle.

A Stage defines:

- objective
- entry criteria
- exit criteria
- techniques
- deliverables
- quality gates

---

## Technique

A professional consulting practice.

Examples:

- Interview
- Workshop
- BPMN
- Fishbone
- SWOT
- Capability Mapping
- Event Storming

Techniques transform information into knowledge.

---

## Activity

A concrete execution of a Technique within a Project.

Examples:

- Interview Product Owner
- Model Payment Process
- Facilitate Workshop

Activities produce Deliverables.

---

## Deliverable Type

A category of business output.

Examples:

- Business Case
- BPMN Diagram
- Requirements Specification
- Capability Map
- Assessment Report
- Training Material

---

## Deliverable

A concrete instance of a Deliverable Type.

Examples:

- Business Case for Crelan AML
- BPMN of Payment Processing
- UAT Test Plan

Deliverables support business decisions.

---

## Review

A structured validation of a Deliverable.

Examples:

- Peer Review
- Quality Review
- Compliance Review
- Architecture Review

---

## Knowledge

Reusable expertise extracted from completed engagements.

Examples:

- templates
- patterns
- lessons learned
- examples
- best practices

Knowledge continuously improves future projects.

---

# Execution Domain

Projects execute Methodologies.

Activities are scheduled and performed.

Deliverables are reviewed.

Knowledge is retained.

---

# AI Domain

AI supports execution.

Agents perform Activities.

The LLM assists Agents.

Knowledge improves AI.

---

# Guiding Principle

Business concepts drive the architecture.

Technology exists to support the business domain, never the other way around.
