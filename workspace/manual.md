# User Manual Requirements Document

## 1. Introduction
### 1.1 Purpose
This document outlines the functional and non-functional requirements for the **User Manual** of [Product/Service Name]. The manual is designed to provide end-users with clear, concise, and actionable guidance to effectively use the product/service.

### 1.2 Scope
The User Manual will cover:
- Product overview and key features
- Installation/configuration instructions
- Step-by-step usage guidance
- Troubleshooting and support information
- Safety and compliance information (if applicable)

### 1.3 Objectives
- Ensure users can operate the product/service without external assistance
- Minimize errors through clear instructions
- Provide a single source of truth for all user-related queries
- Comply with industry standards and regulatory requirements

---

## 2. Stakeholders
| Stakeholder | Role | Requirements |
|-------------|------|--------------|
| End Users | Primary audience | Clear, jargon-free language; step-by-step instructions |
| Technical Support | Secondary audience | Troubleshooting section; contact information |
| Product Managers | Oversight | Alignment with product roadmap; accuracy of feature descriptions |
| Legal/Compliance | Oversight | Compliance with regulations (e.g., GDPR, ADA); safety warnings |

---

## 3. Functional Requirements
### 3.1 Content Structure
- **Front Matter**: Title page, table of contents, disclaimer, and safety information
- **Overview Section**: Product description, key features, and system requirements
- **Installation/Setup**: Detailed steps for installation, configuration, and initial setup
- **Usage Guide**: Step-by-step instructions for core functionalities
- **Troubleshooting**: Common issues, error codes, and resolution steps
- **Appendices**: Glossary, technical specifications, and additional resources

### 3.2 Language and Tone
- Use simple, active voice
- Avoid technical jargon unless necessary; define terms when used
- Include visual aids (diagrams, screenshots, icons) where applicable

---

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements
### 4.1 Usability
- Ensure instructions are logically ordered (e.g., sequential steps, numbered lists)
- Provide contextual help (e.g., tooltips, pop-up explanations)
- Support accessibility standards (e.g., screen reader compatibility)

### 4.2 Accuracy
- All instructions must be validated by QA engineers and product owners
- Include version control information (e.g., "v1.2.0 - Updated 2023-10-05")

### 4.3 Compliance
- Adhere to [relevant standards, e.g., ISO 9241-11, WCAG 2.1]
- Include legal disclaimers and liability limitations

---

## 5. Assumptions and Constraints
### 5.1 Assumptions
- Target audience has basic familiarity with [relevant technology/platform]
- Product/Service APIs and tools are stable and publicly accessible
- No major changes to the product roadmap during manual development

### 5.2 Constraints
- Manual must be completed within [X weeks] due to release deadlines
- Maximum length: [Y pages] to ensure brevity and focus
- No external links to proprietary resources (must include all necessary info)
